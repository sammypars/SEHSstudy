Before you start

Quick review

Demand shifters

  • Tastes / preferences
  • Price of related goods (substitutes, complements)
  • Income (normal vs. inferior goods)
  • Number of buyers
  • Expectations of future prices
A change in the good's own price is a movement along the curve, not a shift.

Supply shifters

  • Input / resource costs
  • Technology
  • Taxes and subsidies
  • Number of sellers
  • Expectations of future prices
  • Weather, natural disasters

ShiftsPQ
D↑ S↑?↑

? = indeterminate. Write each shift's effect separately; the variable where they disagree is the one you can't know.

Disequilibrium

  • Price above equilibrium → Qs > Qd → surplus = Qs − Qd. Price tends to fall.
  • Price below equilibrium → Qd > Qs → shortage = Qd − Qs. Price tends to rise.
  • Price ceiling only matters (is binding) if set below equilibrium. Price floor only matters if set above.
  • Amount actually sold in disequilibrium = the smaller of Qd and Qs.
